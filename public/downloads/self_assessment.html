The Brutal Self-Assessment

A diagnostic tool to identify where you are leaking energy. Be honest.

Rate yourself on a scale of 1 (Never) to 5 (Always).

  1. I use "When I..." statements to delay taking action. (e.g., "When I have more money, I will start"). _____
  2. I organize my life around how I am seen rather than how I want to live. _____
  3. I wait for motivation to strike before executing my daily discipline. _____
  4. I abandon projects entirely if they don't turn out perfect on the first try. _____
  5. I compare my behind-the-scenes reality to everyone else's highlight reels. _____
  6. I negotiate with myself when an alarm goes off or a deadline approaches. _____
  7. I feel like my body is driving the car, and my soul is just sitting in the passenger seat. _____
  8. I let a "flat feeling" dictate the temperature of my entire day. _____

Total Score: _____

The Diagnosis

  • 8 - 15: The Engine is Running. You understand the assignment. You have built the habit of courage. The Momentum 60 challenge will simply pour gasoline on your fire.
  • 16 - 28: The Messy Middle. You start strong but motivation abandons you. You need The But I Will Era. Your immediate fix: Commit to the "Non-Zero Rule" today. If you can't do 100%, do 10%.
  • 29 - 40: Paralyzed by Perfection. You are waiting for confidence that was never going to show up first. You need The Momentum Rule. Your immediate fix: Do one scary thing today before you feel ready. Ship it messy.
