**2. Purpose and audience**
Momentum 60 is a 60-day courage and consistency programme for adults who start programmes and lose motivation. The user picks one small daily action, with a smaller fallback version. The rule is movement every day: one action, however small, keeps the season going. The app adds a daily song, an optional evening reflection, a weekly question and letter, a self-rated wheel at day 1, 30 and 60, and a Steady page with grounding steps and crisis helplines. It is not medical advice.

**3. How to access the main features**
Sign in with the account above. Today tab: choose a practice and tap "I did my action". 60 Days tab: the four-act map. Momentum Book tab: wheel, weekly question, letter, reflections. Top-right menu: Settings, Steady now, Privacy, Account deletion, Sign out.

**4. External services**
- Supabase: sign-in, database, storage of progress and reflections
- Resend: transactional email (password reset, confirmation)
- Apple In-App Purchase / StoreKit: subscription billing in the iOS app
- Stripe: subscription billing on the website only (not in the iOS app)

No AI services are used. No ads or tracking.

**5. Regional differences**

Everything a user writes (practice, reflections, wheel scores) is private to their own account and is never shown to other users, so there is no content to report or block. The partner "Team Up" feature is switched off in this version.
