Day 0: The Setup

Do not start this challenge tomorrow without setting up your environment today. Motivation fades. Your environment is what catches you when it does.

1. Remove the Friction

If you have to think about it, you won't do it. - [ ] Lay out your physical tools (running shoes, notebook, pen) right now. - [ ] Schedule your 5 tasks in your calendar as non-negotiable appointments.

2. Hide the Escapes

  • [ ] Delete or hide your primary distraction apps from your phone's home screen.
  • [ ] Put your phone in another room when executing your tasks.

3. The Non-Zero Rule

If you cannot do the full task, do 10%. A smaller version still counts. Skipping does not. Open the laptop. Put the shoes on. Write one sentence. Do not break the chain.
