# Friction Cheat Sheets
*Print this. Stick it somewhere visible. Use it when your brain tries to talk you out of the work.*

## The 5 Rules Refresher
1. **The Momentum Start:** do one scary or difficult task before you feel ready.
2. **The "But I Will" Habit:** execute your committed daily discipline, even with zero motivation.
3. **The Soul Focus:** 10 minutes of journaling or reflection focused entirely on internal growth.
4. **The Input:** read 10 pages or listen to one Chapter Chat/Song from The Momentum Series.
5. **Zero "When I..." Statements:** catch and eliminate any thought that delays living until conditions are perfect.

## The Excuse Destroyer Protocol
When you hear the excuse, execute the protocol.

| The Excuse | The Protocol |
| :--- | :--- |
| "I'm too tired today." | "Do 10%. Just open the laptop. Just put the shoes on. A smaller version still counts." |
| "I'll make up for it tomorrow." | "Tomorrow doesn't exist. The universe doesn't pause. Momentum is only built today." |
| "It's not perfect." | "Perfect is a delay tactic. Ship it messy." |
| "I don't have time." | "You have the same 24 hours as everyone else. Drop the scrolling. Reclaim 15 minutes." |
| "I don't feel like it." | "Confidence and motivation arrive fashionably late. Act first. Feel later." |
